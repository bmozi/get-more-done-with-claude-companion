# Put a new capability to work

Three connected practice paths accompany Chapters 1, 13, 24–25, 39, 45–46 and 54. All people, interviews, figures and business records here are invented teaching material. None is an actual customer, participant or Claude session.

Choose a path and use its README plus input files. An ordinary text conversation is enough for the core tasks. Work in short sessions; the suggested 15–30 minutes per stage are planning allowances, not measured completion times. You can reason through the inputs on paper, but record that separately from using Claude.

| Your purpose | Begin here | Useful result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Choose a meaningful change and practice it | [Personal growth](personal/README.md) | A chosen action, a rehearsal, a review and a revised next step |
| Develop a skill for work or a new role | [Career](career/README.md) | A checked work sample and an explanation you can give unaided |
| Explore and improve a small service | [Business](business/README.md) | An evidence-linked offer, trial review, delivery plan and cash explanation |

Save attempts with the [record template](attempt-template.md). Record actual outputs before looking at reviewer material. After completing a path, try its changed condition and then a different harmless situation. A reusable method should survive a change of example.

The [capability guide](capability-guide.md) maps these jobs to optional Claude features. You do not need connections, plugins, office add-ins or Code to begin. Reviewer material is stored separately in `reviewer-only/`; do not give it to the assistant or learner during an assessment.
