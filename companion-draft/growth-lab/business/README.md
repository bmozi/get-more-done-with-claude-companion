# Investigate, offer, deliver and learn

Read Chapters 25 and 46; use 11 for research and 12 for wording. All business and financial details are fictional teaching inputs. Use [interview-notes.txt](interview-notes.txt) and [job.json](job.json).

## Discover the problem

Write three questions about a recent participant-materials task before using AI. Ask Claude to improve them without leading the respondent toward buying your service. Use the four supplied notes for the exercise. Separate a stated problem, your proposed explanation, and an unknown. Do not ask AI to simulate customers and call the replies market research.

## Prepare an offer and message

Draft a narrow offer tied to the supplied notes. Explain the benefit in ordinary language. Use the scope in job.json for the delivery exercise. Prepare one sample introduction and a question inviting an honest response. Nothing is sent by this exercise. Identify which statements could be checked and which would require real market evidence.

Read trial T1 in the notes. Record the outcomes without turning requests for examples into sales. Propose one change to investigate next and name a result that would make you reconsider it. Keep alternative explanations visible.

## Check the economics

Calculate the direct-cost remainder, then a separate remainder after the stated labor allowance. Repeat with the six-hour overrun. Ask Claude to explain each distinction, then reproduce the arithmetic yourself. The remainder excludes other costs and is not net profit or an actual recommended price.

## Plan delivery and inspect cash timing

Treat the accepted job as a separate fictional case. Draft a checklist for approved inputs, the draft, client review, the included revision, accessible final files and acceptance. The client then asks for a third page and two additional revisions. Preserve the requested change without automatically accepting it.

Make a four-event cash ledger: deposit, direct cost payment, unpaid final invoice and later receipt. Explain why issuing the invoice changes no cash. Before the later payment, compare available job cash with the stated $80 needed for a new job. State the gap without assuming a new deposit, credit or personal funding.

## Transfer

A prospect wants weekly social posts. List which discovery and review methods transfer, then identify what scope, time, cost and evidence must be learned anew. Keep the original offer distinct from this request.

Keep notes, offer, unsent message, trial interpretation, calculations, delivery checklist and next experiment. No customer contacts, accounting postings or financial transactions are part of this exercise.
