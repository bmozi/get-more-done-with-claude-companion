# Chapter 42 practice guide

Build a playbook for changing situations

## Objective

Translate approved policies into conditional choices, handle overlapping or unknown conditions, and test whether the same evidence leads to a consistent, reviewable response.

## Inputs

- [README.md](../method-labs/playbook/README.md)

## Practice and assessment

Write five request cards: directions from a confirmed participant, three new places, six new places, an unclear count, and a request combining places with an access requirement. Before using an assistant, mark the expected branch or branches from the supplied policy. Then compare its actual classifications and drafts with your answer key.

**Change one condition:** The coordinator reports that three additional places were confirmed elsewhere. Ten of twelve are now confirmed, leaving two. The pending request for three must move to capacity-conflict review. It must not remain eligible on the old five-place snapshot, and the assistant cannot promise two places under B04. Update the policy snapshot and reevaluate dependent pending requests.

**Keep:** Your policy card, decision table, test requests, expected outcomes, observed results when run, and a record of the changed-capacity review. You now have evidence for choosing a response, rather than a collection of persuasive templates.

## Record the attempt

Record date, product, interface, model shown, relevant settings, input version, actual output, checks, corrections, and unresolved questions. Do not put account identifiers or authentication material in the record.
