## Practice and assessment

Design a three-question practice sequence: identify fixed costs, calculate a total, and solve the pack-size variation. Write your answers before asking for feedback.

**Change one condition:** Return to supplies at $2 per participant. The $120 room-hire charge is waived only when attendance is greater than forty; the $30 cleaning charge remains. Before reading the reference, calculate the cost at forty and at forty-one. At forty, the cost is $120 + $30 + $80 = $230. At forty-one, it is $30 + $82 = $112. “Greater than forty” does not include forty. The drop is a consequence of this fictional price rule, not an arithmetic error. This isolated pricing problem assumes adequate room capacity; it does not authorize exceeding any room's limit. Explain the boundary in your own words, then create a different threshold example.

**Keep:** Your unaided attempt, corrected reasoning, and a later transfer problem. A flattering assessment is less useful than evidence of what you can now do.
