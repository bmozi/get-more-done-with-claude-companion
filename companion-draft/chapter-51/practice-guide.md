# Chapter 51 practice guide

Make decisions under uncertainty

## Objective

Separate a supported answer from a guess, challenge a false premise, and choose a check that fits the consequence.

## Inputs

Use your own harmless notes or the self-contained fictional example in the chapter. Live product setup is not preconfigured by this guide.

## Practice and assessment

Create a three-row review log for the claims “the building opens Saturday,” “the fee fits the budget,” and “the room is booked.” Record the source, what it establishes, what it does not establish, and the next action. Your reference result should accept the first two within the exercise's assumptions and leave the booking unresolved.

**Change one condition:** A new record confirms that Room A is reserved, but your quote concerns Room B. Does this resolve the question? No. Match the room identifier before using the record. A document's confident wording does not repair a mismatch.

**Keep:** Your claim log, corrected invitation draft, and one false-premise prompt with a reasoned response. Record whether you designed the response yourself or observed a model produce it.

## Record the attempt

Record date, product, interface, model shown, relevant settings, input version, actual output, checks, corrections, and unresolved questions. Do not put account identifiers or authentication material in the record.
