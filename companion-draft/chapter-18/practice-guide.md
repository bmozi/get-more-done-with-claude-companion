# Chapter 18 practice guide

Capture your know how

## Objective

Turn a repeated practice into a method another person or assistant can follow, including exceptions and judgment points.

## Inputs

- [meeting-notes.txt](../shared/meeting-notes.txt)

## Practice and assessment

Choose a task you have done more than once. Describe the intended result, necessary inputs, recurring choices, exceptions, and final checks. Include one worked example and one situation where the method should stop or ask for help.

**Change one condition:** The input is a brainstorming session with no decisions. A good method should allow an empty decision section. Filling it with invented commitments would be a failure, even if the output looks more substantial.

**Keep:** A method card and a second-input test. You have captured a useful slice of your practice, not cloned your judgment or transferred your experience wholesale into a model.

## Record the attempt

Record date, product, interface, model shown, relevant settings, input version, actual output, checks, corrections, and unresolved questions. Do not put account identifiers or authentication material in the record.
