# Chapter 12 practice guide

Write something that sounds like you

## Objective

Develop a piece from your own ideas, preserve its meaning during revision, and retain a clear record of contributions.

## Inputs

Use your own harmless notes or the self-contained fictional example in the chapter. Live product setup is not preconfigured by this guide.

## Practice and assessment

Write a rough paragraph yourself, or dictate and correct one. Ask Claude for a light edit that preserves every claim, then a structural alternative that reorganizes the argument. Compare both with your source.

Mark which changes you accept and why. You do not have to accept the smoothest version. Sometimes an awkward but specific sentence contains the point a generic rewrite removes.

**Change one condition:** The audience changes from neighbors to venue staff. Revise the explanation for their decision without changing the event's facts.

**Keep:** Your original notes, the AI draft, and your accepted revision. That record supports both continuity and honest attribution.

## Record the attempt

Record date, product, interface, model shown, relevant settings, input version, actual output, checks, corrections, and unresolved questions. Do not put account identifiers or authentication material in the record.
