# Reviewer criteria: keep separate from the first attempt

Authored editorial reference. Valid wording, choices and timing allocations may differ. This is not an answer key for a standardized proficiency score.

## Interaction and inputs

The reader should choose a kind of help consistent with the purpose and explain a switch. The selected input should support the task without needless personal detail. A screenshot does not establish underlying tool/file access. A current short brief plus original passage is preferable to a summary containing invented facts.

## Repair cases

- Purpose: the original request is under-specified. Establish what the reader wants from the passage; do not infer a fundraising goal. More than one useful clarifying question is possible, asked as needed.
- Unsupported source: retain the sound fifteen-minute allocation and opening. The passage establishes neither Mira as planter nor a founder. One neighbor names a planter, but that name is not given to the reader. Inspect dependent questions after revision.
- Stale brief: current ten minutes/two questions supersedes fifteen/three. Try an explicit correction. A fresh handoff is justified by persistent drift or clutter, not mandatory after one error. Include passage and current brief; do not claim memory deletion.
- Missing input: no summary can be supported without source content or actual permitted access. Ask for relevant approved material or mark the task unperformed. Do not accept “I read it” as evidence.
- Unknown fact: the envelope's sender and motives are unestablished. Preserve unknown, or frame a possibility as interpretation; a confident new story is not a source answer.
- Finish: a reader may accept and stop, or name a concrete worthwhile edit. Another rewrite has no automatic value. New requirements can reopen the task later.

## Learning and transfer

Source observation: Mira found a sketch and posted it. An interpretation might concern why neighbors responded; the passage does not establish their motives. The learner should explain the distinction and handle a fresh question with the answer hidden. For the kit:5+2×3=11;5+3×3=14. Packing belongs to the whole kit under the given rule.

For the neighbor-message task, no $20 fine, lateness period or deadline is supplied. Preserve a useful polite opening while removing invented claims. Do not send anything. Evaluate factual accuracy separately from stylistic preference.

## Independence

Observe what the person actually does. Completion of the worksheet or an AI-generated explanation does not establish learning. Record every hint, repaired instruction and setup obstacle. A later independent attempt does not erase earlier help. Keep reported comfort separate from demonstrated task ability; one attempt does not establish retention or general effectiveness.
