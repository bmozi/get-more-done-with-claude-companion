# Chapter 47 practice guide

Run an automation and recover from failure

## Objective

Follow a recurring task from trigger to checked output, distinguish a missed run from a failed run, and recover without concealing what happened.

## Inputs

- [README.md](../recovery-lab/README.md)
- [maintenance.json](../recovery-lab/maintenance.json)

## Practice and assessment

Create a paper ledger for the ordinary November 2 run, the failed November 9 attempt, its late recovery, and a duplicate trigger. Record the key, attempt number, source date, status, and final output location or reason no output exists.

**Change one condition:** H2's due date becomes November 2 before the first run. The checked output now has two due tasks, H1 and H2. H4 remains unknown; H3 remains excluded. Then imagine processing stops after saving a temporary draft. The period must remain incomplete and eligible for controlled recovery.

**Keep:** The contract, input version, proposed ledger, expected outputs, recovery rule, and stop checklist. Label paper work as designed. If you run the companion, retain the actual commands and ledger separately; do not relabel them as a Claude or scheduler trial.

## Record the attempt

Record date, product, interface, model shown, relevant settings, input version, actual output, checks, corrections, and unresolved questions. Do not put account identifiers or authentication material in the record.

Status: local deterministic recovery reference executed with controlled faults; no scheduler, Claude execution, power-failure or human-learning trial.
