# Chapter 54 practice guide

Explore what you do not yet know

## Objective

Use AI to discover unfamiliar questions and concepts, examine assumptions, build a learning path, and test an idea beyond the conversation.

## Inputs

- [Connected personal practice](../growth-lab/personal/README.md)
Use your own harmless notes or the self-contained fictional example in the chapter. Live product setup is not preconfigured by this guide.

## Practice and assessment

Use the fictional library figures. Produce a one-page problem map and explain the registration-to-attendance gap without assigning a cause. Select one hypothesis and propose a small observation that could weaken it. Then explain the concept of a denominator without assistance.

**Change one condition:** A later source says the twelve registrations include four cancellations recorded before the event. Eight active registrants attended. The earlier apparent attendance gap disappears under that definition. Correct the map and investigate another question rather than defending your first explanation. Do not infer that all access barriers have vanished.

**Keep:** Your first question, revised map, source checks, unaided explanation, and one bounded next experiment. You are learning to enter unfamiliar territory with questions you can improve and claims you can examine.
