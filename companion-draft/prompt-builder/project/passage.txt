At the community garden, Mira found an unmarked envelope beside a tray of seedlings. Inside was a sketch of the garden as it had looked twenty years earlier. She put the sketch on the noticeboard and wrote, “Who remembers this?” By evening, three neighbors had added notes. One named the person who planted the old apple tree. Another drew a path that no longer existed. The third left a question: “What should we plant next?”
