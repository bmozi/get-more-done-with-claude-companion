# Read the promise before assigning the work

Fictional meeting W. Source:

W1: Noor: We could add a second demonstration.
W2: The group agrees to keep one demonstration this week.
W3: Jules: I will prepare the equipment checklist.
W4: No deadline was agreed for that checklist.

Expected meaning:

| Meeting | Status | Statement | Evidence IDs | Owner | Due date |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| W | Proposal | Add a second demonstration | W1 | Not assigned | Not set |
| W | Decision | Keep one demonstration this week | W2 | Not assigned | Not set |
| W | Assigned action | Prepare the equipment checklist | W3, W4 | Jules | Not set |

The word "could" offers a possibility. The group's agreement establishes the decision. "I will" establishes ownership, but the notes supply no deadline. The owner field belongs to the work, not merely to whoever spoke. These interpretations should survive a change in names or table wording.

If W5 later states "Jules cannot prepare it; Noor agrees to take over," update the current action to Noor and cite W5 with W3/W4. Do not keep two current owners. If W5 merely says "Noor thinks Jules should finish it tomorrow," that is not an accepted reassignment or agreed deadline. Ask what the new line actually establishes before changing the record.
