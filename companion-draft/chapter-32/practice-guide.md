# Chapter 32 practice guide

Give specialists clear jobs

## Objective

Divide work among specialists only when the boundaries help, and integrate findings without confusing multiple opinions with independent proof.

## Inputs

Use your own harmless notes or the self-contained fictional example in the chapter. Live product setup is not preconfigured by this guide.

## Practice and assessment

Write three bounded review assignments for one chapter: factual claims, learning sequence, and visual explanation. Perform them sequentially or with supported separate workers. Require findings with evidence and distinguish defects from preferences.

**Change one condition:** Two reviewers recommend incompatible changes. Write a short integration decision explaining the reader consequence of each option and why one was selected. Retain the useful rejected alternative when it could matter later.

**Keep:** The assignments, findings, and integrated revision record. Additional workers should reduce a real bottleneck or improve coverage, not merely make the process look more elaborate.

## Record the attempt

Record date, product, interface, model shown, relevant settings, input version, actual output, checks, corrections, and unresolved questions. Do not put account identifiers or authentication material in the record.

Status: exercise design; live Claude execution and human learning trial not performed. The separate maintenance reference test log covers only that local program.
