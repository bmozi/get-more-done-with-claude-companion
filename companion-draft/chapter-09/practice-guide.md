# Chapter 9 practice guide

Understand memory and private information

## Objective

Distinguish conversation history, remembered preferences, current context, and data handling before sharing information.

## Inputs

Use your own harmless notes or the self-contained fictional example in the chapter. Live product setup is not preconfigured by this guide.

## Practice and assessment

Use a harmless fictional preference, such as “use metric units for this practice project.” Inspect what the available controls say about where the preference is stored, whether it applies outside the project, and how to change it. Record observed behavior separately from the interface description. Mark unavailable controls or storage behavior you cannot observe as unknown; a plausible answer from Claude is not a storage audit.

**Change one condition:** The next task needs inches. Give that requirement explicitly and check the result. A useful preference should support the task, not override its facts.

**Keep:** A short data-handling note for your project: what information is necessary, where it may be used, and which controls you checked.

## Record the attempt

Record date, product, interface, model shown, relevant settings, input version, actual output, checks, corrections, and unresolved questions. Do not put account identifiers or authentication material in the record.
