# Try the revised reader paths

Prepared October 30, 2026. No human trial has occurred. Follow the consent, assistance and privacy rules in [README.md](README.md). This file prepares a practical editorial trial; it does not authorize contacting people. Give each willing participant only their route packet and chapters. Withhold `growth-lab/reviewer-only/` and editorial notes until their first attempt is recorded.

| Route | Learner receives | First observable result | Transfer check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

Before the attempt ask what the reader wants to learn and which setup is familiar. Let them use a real available Claude account with fictional inputs, or record a paper-only attempt explicitly. Do not equate successful account setup with learning.

Record the chapter/source commit, actual materials, first output, time only if measured, the reader's explanation, stumbling points and every intervention. Ask which question helped them discover something and which explanation remained unclear. These are reported reactions, not standardized learning scores.

Hide the worked answer for the changed condition. Ask the person to explain the reasoning. If they request help, provide it and record the assistance. A later unaided attempt can be informative but does not erase the earlier intervention. Use the separate reviewer criteria to judge source consistency; allow valid alternative actions.

Repair observed defects and offer a new attempt only if the person wishes. Record what remains untested, including accessibility and retention. Do not convert three prepared routes into three successful trials.
