# Chapter 3 practice guide

Choose where to work

## Objective

Choose a working environment for a task and record enough information to reproduce an exercise.

## Inputs

Use your own harmless notes or the self-contained fictional example in the chapter. Live product setup is not preconfigured by this guide.

## Practice and assessment

Write a setup card: date, product, interface, model shown, plan, relevant settings, and tools available. Omit account identifiers and private information. Then name the smallest environment that can complete your next task.

**Change one condition:** The task must run while your laptop is closed. What execution environment would actually remain available? A remote view of a local session is not necessarily a remotely hosted worker. Verify where the work runs before promising unattended completion.

**Keep:** The setup card and your reason for choosing that environment. A specialist product such as Claude Science belongs in this map when the reader's research requires it; it does not replace the subject knowledge needed to evaluate a scientific claim.

## Record the attempt

Record date, product, interface, model shown, relevant settings, input version, actual output, checks, corrections, and unresolved questions. Do not put account identifiers or authentication material in the record.
