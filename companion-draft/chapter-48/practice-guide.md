# Chapter 48 practice guide

Coordinate subagents on a real project

## Objective

Divide a project into bounded assignments, preserve independent findings, and integrate disagreements against shared evidence.

## Inputs

- [README.md](../coordination-lab/README.md)

## Practice and assessment

Write the shared packet, the two independent review assignments, and one integration record. Check both outputs directly before creating the communication draft. Record whether you used paper reasoning, separate chats, or actual supported subagents.

**Change one condition:** One additional guest confirms. Occupancy becomes eleven. River still qualifies with one spare place and a $400 total. Garden now fails capacity, even though it remains cheaper. Under the unchanged fixed-cost assumptions, River becomes the recommendation. The communication draft must change with it.

**Keep:** The packet version, assignments, original findings, corrections, integration decision, and final draft. Measure setup and review effort before deciding that parallel work helped this small project.

## Record the attempt

Record date, product, interface, model shown, relevant settings, input version, actual output, checks, corrections, and unresolved questions. Do not put account identifiers or authentication material in the record.

Status: actual AI subagent trial on fictional data; not Claude-specific, a timing comparison or human learning evidence.
