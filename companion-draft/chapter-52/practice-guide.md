# Chapter 52 practice guide

Manage information across AI tools

## Objective

Build an inspectable information handoff, retain source ownership and attribution, and track what each tool receives.

## Inputs

Use your own harmless notes or the self-contained fictional example in the chapter. Live product setup is not preconfigured by this guide.

## Practice and assessment

Write the original subtraction packet, then revise it for the cleaning charge. Create a two-row ledger showing which version supports which result. Verify that no contact information or staffing note entered either packet.

**Change one condition:** The next job is to prepare a staffing schedule. Does that justify sending every private note? No. Identify the specific availability constraints needed, whether you may share them, and a less revealing representation where appropriate. If permission or meaning remains unclear, retain the question rather than assuming consent.

**Keep:** Both packet versions, a field-selection explanation, the source references, and a correction record. Success means the recipient has enough authorized information to do the named job without inheriting unrelated material.

## Record the attempt

Record date, product, interface, model shown, relevant settings, input version, actual output, checks, corrections, and unresolved questions. Do not put account identifiers or authentication material in the record.
