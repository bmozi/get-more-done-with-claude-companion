# Chapter 44 practice guide

Connect prompts without losing evidence

## Objective

Pass checked work between prompts, preserve source facts and unresolved questions, and repair a failed step without disguising the failure in polished prose.

## Inputs

- [Plan and carry out a small project](../prompt-builder/project/README.md)
- [README.md](../method-labs/prompt-chain/README.md)

## Practice and assessment

Run the three stages with the supplied packet. Save each raw result, your checks, and any correction. Compare the whole process with one well-specified request given the same packet. Count transfer and checking time, not just generation time. If the chain adds no useful control or quality, the simpler request may serve this task better.

**Change one condition:** B2 becomes 11 people. Pine now fails capacity; Elm still exceeds budget. The correct result is that neither venue meets both constraints. Preserve the budget and headcount instead of inventing an extra chair or quietly dropping materials. Identify a concrete next decision, such as seeking another venue or asking whether a constraint may change.

**Keep:** The original packet, versioned handoffs, calculations, final explanation, and evidence for choosing either the chain or the single-request baseline.

## Record the attempt

Record date, product, interface, model shown, relevant settings, input version, actual output, checks, corrections, and unresolved questions. Do not put account identifiers or authentication material in the record.

Status: local deterministic reference executed; live Claude execution and human learning trial not performed. See method-lab evidence for tested cases and limits.
