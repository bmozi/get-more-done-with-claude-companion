# Chapter 36 practice guide

Finish a capstone someone else can use

## Objective

Solve a real need of your own, use AI to learn the parts you do not yet understand, and adapt the result for someone else without assuming their needs match yours.

## Inputs

- [personal-to-shared-map.md](../capstone-lab/personal-to-shared-map.md)

## Practice and assessment

Complete the personal version first. Demonstrate one useful task, explain its evidence, and perform a changed example without relying on an unexplained answer. Then identify one possible recipient and investigate their needs before adapting the work. Conduct a self-check and, when an appropriate reader is available, an independent attempt. Repair material gaps and repeat the affected task.

**Change one condition:** The original creator is unavailable for a month and the group cannot access the AI service. The next person must find the current records, distinguish unknown information, identify the responsible coordinator, and use the fallback. If they cannot, revise the handoff before expanding the project.

**Keep:** The personal and adapted versions, capstone map, needs evidence, permissions, trial observations, maintenance owner, and an honest readiness statement. “Local tests passed; group handoff untested” names what remains to learn.

## Record the attempt

Record date, product, interface, model shown, relevant settings, input version, actual output, checks, corrections, and unresolved questions. Do not put account identifiers or authentication material in the record.

Status: exercise design; live Claude execution and human learning trial not performed. The separate maintenance reference test log covers only that local program.
