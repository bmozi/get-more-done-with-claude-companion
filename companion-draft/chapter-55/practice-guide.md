# Chapter 55 practice guide

Anticipate AI and test new possibilities

## Objective

Turn evidence about changing AI capabilities into explicit forecasts, competing scenarios and small experiments that improve your choices today.

## Inputs

- [forecast-card.md](../foresight-lab/forecast-card.md)

## Practice and assessment

Choose one recurring task. Save two dated signals from primary sources, one explicit forecast for a stated date, its assumptions, and a competing scenario. Name an observation that would make you revise the forecast. Design a fictional-data trial with a useful outcome even if your prediction is wrong.

For the family-gathering example, begin with preparing a draft from one supplied export. Measure corrections and review time; keep all failed attempts. Expand to changed records only after understanding those errors. Do not count an attractive demonstration as proof that the whole workflow is ready.

**Change one condition:** The model improves, but your organization cannot connect its participant records. Revise the plan around a permitted export or fictional practice data. Explain that the fictional-record forecast may still hold, while deployment with real participant records is constrained. Keep these claims separate. Do not quietly redefine access as solved.

**Keep:** A forecast card, sources, alternative scenario, disconfirming signal, review date and a bounded experiment. Your ability to see possibilities grows when you learn what to notice, what to question and what to try.

## Record the attempt

Record date, product, interface, model shown, relevant settings, input version, actual output, checks, corrections, and unresolved questions. Do not put account identifiers or authentication material in the record.
