# Chapter 49 practice guide

Red-team a recommendation and its evidence

## Objective

Challenge a recommendation with specific counterexamples, trace disputed claims to sources, and repair the reasoning without pretending that every objection deserves equal weight.

## Inputs

Use your own harmless notes or the self-contained fictional example in the chapter. Live product setup is not preconfigured by this guide.

## Practice and assessment

Create five checks, one for each review area above. Apply them to the flawed recommendation, write the correction, and rerun the checks. Preserve the original answer so someone can see what improved.

**Change one condition:** A November 10 Pine renewal sets the price at $0.55 per handout plus $20 delivery, remains valid through November 13, and promises delivery by November 18. Pine now totals $130 and meets the requirements. Under Q5, it becomes the preferred option, saving $5 against Cedar. Ordering still requires approval. If your repaired answer cannot change when the evidence changes, the review has become attachment to a verdict.

**Keep:** The source packet, claim list, accepted and rejected findings, repaired recommendation, and retest. Label the exercise as designed practice until actual outputs and observations are captured.

## Record the attempt

Record date, product, interface, model shown, relevant settings, input version, actual output, checks, corrections, and unresolved questions. Do not put account identifiers or authentication material in the record.
