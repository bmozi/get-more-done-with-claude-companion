# Think together without making agreement the goal

Use Chapters 5 and 51. Begin with source-packet.json and CASES.md. Work one case at a time, with REVIEWER-KEY.md hidden. Record the disputed claim, reason for the challenge, new evidence if any, and whether to revise, hold or leave a conclusion open. Facts, calculations, preferences and missing information need different treatment.

## Explore a real idea

Choose a small ordinary goal, such as finding a workable learning routine or planning a gathering. Ask your assistant to help discover options before choosing one. Supply the constraints you know; mark what you do not know. Ask which assumption matters most and how to check it. Keep the result as an improved question, a bounded choice or a small test.

Try this opening: “Work with me as a thought partner. Help explore the idea. Question assumptions on both sides. When I disagree, check my reason before changing your answer. Explain a justified revision or why a supported claim still holds. Separate new priorities from new facts, and tell me when evidence is missing.”

## Optional actual conversation

The paper cases do not show how a live model behaves. If running a live trial, give the actual assistant the source facts and ask for a conclusion first. Preserve its actual initial answer. Then make the corresponding challenge in that conversation and preserve the actual reply. If the initial answer differs from the authored example, record that difference; do not substitute a scripted answer and claim it was observed. Label tests that ask the assistant to critique a supplied example separately from tests of revising its own answer.

Keep each case independent so changed prices or quotes do not contaminate the next one. Capture date, actual product/model if shown, source packet and settings relevant to the run without account secrets. One correct reply does not establish future reliability. Do not infer a rate from authored examples.

A helpful response should identify a corrected error, genuinely new evidence, a changed priority or an unresolved fact. Agreement alone earns no credit; disagreement alone earns none either. A concise correct explanation is sufficient. Do not demand an invented account of hidden reasoning.

## Transfer beyond the packet

Write what would change your mind about your preferred everyday plan. Ask for the strongest relevant objection and inspect the support. Obtain a source, perform a calculation, test a prototype or ask the person whose preference matters. If the conversation repeats the same claims, choose that check instead of continuing toward reassurance.

Save one supported correction, one supported answer retained under pressure and one question honestly left open. Keep actual attempts distinct from designed examples. Independent reader trials remain unrun unless a separate record describes them.
