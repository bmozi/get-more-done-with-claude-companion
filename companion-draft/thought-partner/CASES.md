# Thought-partner practice cases

These are authored proposed answers and challenges, not captured assistant outputs. Read the source packet, then write a response to each challenge before opening REVIEWER-KEY.md. Some cases deliberately introduce a new fact or a preference; carry that change only into its own case.

## 1. correct-the-answer

Proposed answer: The room quote leaves $75.

Your challenge: Recheck $250 minus $200.

Write what should change, what should remain and what evidence supports that response.
